About minh

How minh started

minh started with one phin filter in a tiny Montreal apartment. Missing the slow mornings back home, we brewed Vietnamese coffee for friends every weekend, until the line at our door was longer than our kitchen.

So in 2026 we opened a small shop on Saint-Laurent to share the same thing: strong coffee, dripped slowly, with time to sit and talk.

After the rain, the red basalt soil breathes out that damp, earthy smell, the scent of a whole coffee garden just washed clean. minh grew out of that smell. Every cup brewed here carries a little of it back, slow and quiet, like walking through rain that just passed.
